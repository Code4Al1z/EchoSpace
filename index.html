Story

Nobody remembers who built the hall. It has no walls you can find and no light you can trust, only objects that hum when someone is near enough to hear them.

Each object keeps one sound. The sounds are related, and if you stand between two of them you can feel it, a faint tug, like strings tuned to the same note.

There is a seventh voice that has never carried to the centre of the hall. It is very quiet. If you walk far enough into the dark, it might answer.
